# Data and background jobs

Where the Worker runs, forecasts, rating, past-weather seasons, background jobs and outside data. Moved verbatim from the root `CLAUDE.md`. Paths are from the repository root.

- **Where it runs:** D1's primary is in eastern North America (ENAM) with no read replicas, so `[placement] region = "aws:us-east-1"` in `wrangler.toml` runs page requests next to it (the `cf-placement` response header says where). Files under `[assets]` still come from the nearest location, and the cron is not moved.
- **Weather:** Open-Meteo API, free, no key needed. `getAtmosphericProfile` (`src/lib/weather-profile.ts`) is the one forecast fetch: it always fetches and caches 7 days per place (a 7-day call costs Open-Meteo quota the same as 1 day), returns them from the place's local today, and retries once on 429. It serves the place's newest cached copy, whatever its UTC fetch date. The hourly cron first refreshes saved sites' forecasts before they expire (`/api/forecasts/refresh`, `app/lib/forecast-refresh.server.ts`, at most 60 places a run) and deletes copies over two days old. A copy is stored gzipped in `data_gz` (`src/lib/gzip-json.ts`: about 60 KB instead of 450 KB of JSON), with `{}` in `data`; copies from before that keep the JSON in `data`. Call `setProfileDb(db)` before using it. `profileToday(profile)` is the site's local date; never use UTC `toISOString()` for "today".
- **Rating:** `src/lib/forecast-engine` is the only place a day is rated (gates, window, quality, hazards), and it also says what flight the window gives (`flight`: sled, ridge, thermal, xc) and whether the day meets the pilot's goal (`goalMet`). Model rain only counts where it has proved right: in the tropics (`rainTrust` in `gates.ts`) only heavy showers count, and `rainNote` says what was set aside. Storms count only with showers within `RAIN_SOON_HOURS` (`stormGate`; plain unstable air passes and `stormNote` says so). Bump `ENGINE_VERSION` (`forecast-engine/index.ts`) when a change re-rates past days: the season build then rebuilds every season.
- **Model verification:** `scripts/verify/` and the "Model verification pull" workflow pull day-ahead forecasts from many models at airports (Open-Meteo Previous Runs API) to score against METARs; results land on `refs/model-verify/latest`. It runs on GitHub because Open-Meteo rate-limits Claude's sandbox.
- **Seasons:** the season build (`jobs/season-build.ts`, a Workflow in the jobs Worker, started twice a day by its cron) rates every day of the last `SEASON_YEARS` (20) years of whole months through the forecast engine on ERA5, ECMWF's record of past weather, read from Open-Meteo's open copy on Amazon (`src/lib/season-build/era5.ts`, `@openmeteo/file-reader`; CC BY 4.0, no account, no rate limit; `era5-profile.ts` turns it into a profile in local time: surface fields, the 100 m wind as the one level above ground, no CAPE or visibility, so storm and fog gates never fire on past days, and a day with fewer than 20 complete hours, such as Jan to mid-June 2024 when the copy has no boundary layer height, isn't rated). Only each site's time zone comes from Open-Meteo's API. It does this for every followed site, every custom site and the travel launches in `src/lib/destinations.ts` (`AREAS`: our own hand-picked flying areas such as Southern Spain or Cauca Valley, each a few launches within `AREA_RADIUS_KM` (100 km) of its middle, a rule `destinations.test.ts` checks; each launch is rated at the listed site it finds in pgsites). It writes each site to `site_seasons` (`src/lib/seasons.ts`), keyed by site and launch settings but not the goal: `summary` is a JSON `SeasonStats` over the whole span (each goal's month counts, and rated and go days as one bit a day, about 7 KB, so pages join an area's launches day by day with `anyGoSharesFromStats` and get each month's middle half of years with `statsSpread`), and `days` keeps only the last `SEASON_MONTHS` (24) months day by day for checks against flown days. Over those months `winds` keeps the wind at the launch each hour from 9:00 to 18:00 local (`launchWinds` in `src/lib/season-build/core.ts`, `encodeDayWinds`, about 21 KB a row) for the experiment below; a row without it is due again, and pages read it only through `seasonWinds`. `source` is `era5`; older rows (null source, all days in `days`, no summary) are read through `seasonStats(decodeSeasonDays(...))` until the build rates them again (`rowStats` in `app/lib/seasons.server.ts`, which selects the day list only for those). Each ERA5 cell is a step per five years (`BLOCK_YEARS`; its launches share one read, about a minute since a Worker waits on six connections at a time) and a step that joins and writes them, each tried three times; a cell that still fails waits for the next run. A Worker has 128 MB: reading all 20 years and rating a year at once ran out of it, so `rateSite` rates a local month at a time (same days as a year at a time). The run records itself in `app_state` (`season_build_last_run`), which the health check reads. The "Cloudflare job" GitHub workflow only starts a run by hand (`job`, `dry_run`, `limit`) and prints its steps. Site pages show a Season card (with a line per month for the middle half of years, and an ERA5 credit, `SeasonCredit`) and a "this week against a typical week" line; `/planning` (old `/year` links redirect) has Where to fly (areas or countries ranked, where a day is a go when any of the area's launches was, `anyGoShares`; `?area=`/`?country=` opens one for its launches; a world map, `src/components/planning-map.tsx`, shows every area colored by the window, rated or not), Favorites (followed and private sites) and Comps, for any start month up to five years out and a window of a month to two years: one month reads as a ranked list, longer windows as a month-by-month grid. Season data never changes a verdict. Note pgsites `sites.country_code` is uppercase and comps' is lowercase.
- **Cross-country flights:** NOGA's map of cross-country takeoffs (noga.es/paragliding/map, built from XContest flights of 100 points or more, 2015 to 2025, counts only) is copied weekly into `noga_takeoffs` by the `noga-sync` Workflow (`jobs/noga-sync.ts`), recorded in `app_state` (`noga_sync_last_run`, read by the health check). Takeoffs group into NOGA's sites and sites into regions (`src/lib/noga.ts`). A launch is on the map when a NOGA takeoff is within `NOGA_NEAR_KM` (3 km): site pages (listed and private) show its NOGA site's flights by month in a "Cross-country flights" card (`src/components/xc-flights.tsx`, `nogaFlightsAt` in `app/lib/noga.server.ts`). Planning shows flights a year in each month for launches (their NOGA site) and areas and countries (the NOGA regions their launches are in), and can rank by them (`sort=xc`). Credit NOGA and XContest (`NogaCredit`) and link NOGA's own pages. Never show or store a pilot or a flight link. It never changes a verdict.
- **Background jobs** run on Cloudflare, never on GitHub: the `soarcast-jobs` Worker (`jobs/`, `jobs/wrangler.toml`, deployed by the deploy workflow after the site) holds Cloudflare Workflows its crons start (`JOBS` in `jobs/index.ts`: the season build twice a day, the NOGA copy on Mondays, the ensembles each morning; a cron skips while its last run is still going). `jobs/own-sites.ts` lists the followed and private sites the season build and the ensembles rate. It is its own Worker so the site never loads the jobs' code (the ERA5 reader is 2 MB of WebAssembly, and the site has a startup CPU limit). It binds SoarCast's D1 and pgsites' D1 (read only). The reader fetches its WebAssembly at runtime, which a Worker can't, so `[alias]` points `@openmeteo/file-format-wasm` at `jobs/om-wasm.ts`, which starts the bundled module. That module asks for a fixed 64 MB of memory, half a Worker's 128 MB, and the season build ran out of memory with it: `[build]` runs `jobs/shrink-wasm.mjs` first, which writes a copy with 16 MB to `jobs/.build` (a read needs under 4 MB; the memory can't grow, as the reader keeps its first view of it). Run wrangler for the jobs Worker from the repository root. A Workflow's steps can share one invocation, and an ERA5 cell's 20 years take about 8,500 requests, so `[limits] subrequests` is raised well past the default 10,000. Test locally with `npx wrangler dev -c jobs/wrangler.toml --test-scheduled` and `/__scheduled`. New background jobs go here as Workflows. GitHub runs tests, deploys and checks started by hand (the flown-day check and model verification hit Open-Meteo's API, which rate-limits Claude's sandbox).
- **Flown days:** Syncing Pilots (github.com/mckinley/syncing-pilots, live inReach/SPOT tracking, same Cloudflare account) says how many pilots flew from a launch each local day, by kind (sled, soared, xc). SoarCast calls its `FlownDaysApi` over the `SYNCING_PILOTS` service binding (`[[services]]` in `wrangler.toml`, no URL or key; missing in local dev). `flownDaysAt` (`app/lib/flown-days.server.ts`) asks for the start of the month two years back to today, keeps it in the `flown_days` table and refetches after `FLOWN_REFRESH_HOURS`; it never fails a page. `src/lib/flown-days.ts` checks the days against a site's season (`checkAgainstFlown`) and shares them by month. Site pages stream it in (a loader promise behind `<Await>`): the "Flown here" card (`src/components/flown-here.tsx`) and a Flown column on the Season card. `/api/flown-days?site=<pgsites id>` gives the same check for every goal, for checking the engine across sites. Flown days never change a verdict. The shape of the answer is Syncing Pilots' `app/lib/flown-days.ts`: change both together. Its `upNow` on the same binding (`upNowAt`, `app/lib/up-now.server.ts`, asked live on each page load, never kept) gives pilots in the air within `UP_NOW_AIR_KM` now, pilots on launch and today so far. The "Up now" card (`src/components/up-now.tsx`, `src/lib/up-now.ts`) streams in at the top of site pages and shows nothing when nobody flew yet. Its shape is Syncing Pilots' `app/lib/up-now.ts`. The "Flown-day check" workflow (`scripts/seasons/flown-check.ts`, started by hand) re-rates the last two years at launches with 10+ flown days and prints how the ratings did and what the engine saw on days below Go, as counts only (public log). A run on main saves its ratings as the baseline; a run on a branch compares an engine change with it day by day (days moved up to Go or down, each weighed against the launch's Go days in the same month). Archived forecasts stay in the Actions cache, so a re-run spends no Open-Meteo calls.
