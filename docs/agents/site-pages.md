# Site pages and features

What site pages, the dashboard, planning and maps show, and where it comes from. Moved verbatim from the root `CLAUDE.md`. Paths are from the repository root.

- **Flying now (OGN):** the Up now card also counts the paragliders, hang gliders and gliders the Open Glider Network sees in the air within `UP_NOW_AIR_KM` (`ognNowAt`, `app/lib/ogn.server.ts`, one edge-cached minute per place), and the map's Flying now layer draws those in view from the browser every `FLYING_NOW_REFRESH_SECONDS`. Both read OGN's live map (`live.glidernet.org/lxml.php`, rules in `src/lib/ogn.ts`), which already drops no-track aircraft. Never keep a position or show who flies (no name, registration or competition number). OGN data is ODbL: credit it. It never changes a verdict.
- **Wind now:** site pages (listed and private) stream a "Wind now" card: up to 4 stations within 50 km with a fresh reading (90 min), nearest first, with direction, average, gust, age, distance and height against the launch. winds.mobi's API (`https://winds.mobi/api/2.3/stations/`, no key, CORS open) gathers METARs, OpenWindMap/Pioupiou, FFVL, MeteoSwiss and more. Rules in `src/lib/wind-now.ts`, the fetch in `app/lib/wind-now.server.ts` (edge cache 3 min, never fails a page), the card in `src/components/wind-now.tsx`. The map's Wind now layer asks winds.mobi from the browser for the stations in view. Holfuy stations are left out (Holfuy allows its readings only in its own widgets). Never changes a verdict.
- **Track record:** site pages show how the day-before forecast did at the nearest airport over the last year (`ForecastTrackRecord`, `/api/track-record`). `src/lib/track-record.ts` scores it and rates rain and wind Good/Fair/Poor; `app/lib/track-record.server.ts` finds the airport (aviationweather.gov station list, within 60 km), pulls Open-Meteo Previous Runs and the IEM METAR archive, and keeps each airport's record in D1 for a month.
- **Goals:** every site has a kind (thermal, ridge, both, sled; from Paragliding Earth's `is_thermal`/`is_soaring`/`is_xc` tags, or the pilot's own `site_kind`) and a goal (`launch` safe launch is enough, `soar`, `great`; `src/lib/site-goals.ts`). Screens show Go / Flyable / Marginal / Not flyable from `src/lib/verdict-display.ts`, never the 0-100 score, and alerts fire only on days that are a go. Listed sites become a `Site` through `launchSiteToSite` (`src/lib/launch-sites.ts`); `refreshLaunchSites` re-copies stale ones from pgsites.
- **Your own sites:** `custom_sites` are private to their owner: load them with `findOwnedCustomSite` (`app/lib/custom-sites.server.ts`) and turn them into a `Site` with `customSiteToSite` (`src/lib/custom-sites.ts`), so pages and alerts rate them through the same engine. Add/edit pages are `/sites/custom/new` and `/sites/custom/:id/edit`. `/api/terrain` gives the form the launch elevation, a launch direction suggested from the slopes around the pin (`src/lib/terrain.ts`, Open-Meteo elevation API) and listed sites nearby.
- **Areas:** saved sites (favorites and private sites) are grouped into the pilot's own areas (`site_areas`, `area_id` on `user_favorite_sites` and `custom_sites`; `src/lib/site-areas.ts`, `app/lib/site-areas.server.ts`). `syncAreas` runs in the loaders that list saved sites: a site with no area joins the nearest saved site's area within `AREA_JOIN_KM` (150 km) or starts one, and empty areas go. The dashboard shows one area (`?area=<id>`, or `all` for one row per area, a day is a go when any site is), by default the one nearest the request (`context.cloudflare.cf`, passed in by `worker.ts`), and loads only that area's forecasts. Settings renames, moves, joins and mutes areas; alerts skip muted areas. Planning's "Save this area" follows a flying area's launches into an area of the same name.
- **Comps:** pgsites collects FAI's paragliding calendar (CIVL Cat 1 and 2) plus World Cup rounds and serves `/api/comps` and `/api/comps/near` (`getComps`, `compsNear` in `src/lib/pgsites-client.ts`). `app/lib/comps.server.ts` fetches them and never fails a page over them; `src/lib/comps.ts` has the dates, levels and regions. A comp within 25 km (`COMP_RADIUS_KM`) is held at a site: the site page shows a line on the day card and a "Comps here" card with its history, the dashboard marks comp days with a trophy, and `/planning` lists comps worldwide for the chosen months. Comps never change a verdict. Comp pages on Airtribune, CIVL Comps and PWCA are linked, never fetched.
- **Experiments:** ideas still being tested, shown only to signed-in pilots who switch on "Show experiments" in Settings (`settings.show_experiments`, `showsExperiments` in `app/lib/experiments.server.ts`), under an Experiments heading at the foot of site pages (`ExperimentsSection`). The Guide's Experiments page (`src/guide/pages/experiments.md`) says what each one is, how it was tested and what the test found. They never change a verdict. An idea that tested weak can still ship here. The first is "Wind on flown days" (`src/lib/flown-winds.ts`, `src/components/flown-winds.tsx`): for each compass direction, the days the season's ERA5 wind came from there at the hour most flights started, and how many pilots flew (Syncing Pilots), against the listed directions. Learning per-launch directions from flown days for the rating was tested at 41 launches and didn't hold from one year to the next. "How sure" (`src/lib/ensembles.ts`, `src/components/how-sure.tsx`): ECMWF's 51-run ensemble (Open-Meteo's ensemble API, `ecmwf_ifs025`, 10 days) rated run by run through the engine at the launch. The runs have no boundary layer height (no ensemble on Open-Meteo has it), so only the gate verdict counts: how many runs give a flyable window, beside the main forecast. The `ensembles` Workflow in the jobs Worker (`jobs/ensembles.ts`, cron 08:10 UTC) fetches it once a day for followed and private sites, one fetch per 0.25° square (about 56 Open-Meteo calls each: calls weigh variables times runs over ten), and writes `site_ensembles` (read by `howSureExperiment`) and `ensemble_history` (each day's counts by days ahead, kept 800 days, for scoring it against flown days later). Open-Meteo's open copy keeps only the ensembles' precipitation probability, not the runs. "Terrain around the launch" (`src/lib/terrain-study`, `src/components/terrain-study.tsx`): the slope at the pin, the higher ground upwind for each wind direction (a lee closer than `LEE_RATIO` times its height) and when the sun is on the launch, from the Copernicus GLO-30 heights read straight from their open copy on Amazon (`dem.ts`: each 1° GeoTIFF's header, then only the 1024-pixel squares a window touches, inflated with `DecompressionStream` and the float predictor undone; 30 m within 1.5 km, the 60 m overview out to 15 km, about 5 to 10 MB a pin). The first look at a pin makes the study (`terrainStudyAt`, `app/lib/terrain-study.server.ts`) and `site_terrain` keeps it for good, keyed by the pin to 4 decimals; bump `TERRAIN_VERSION` to make them again. The slope faced within 45° of a listed wind at 84% of 250 listed launches.
- **Maps:** Leaflet via react-leaflet, use `React.lazy` + `Suspense` (no SSR). Every map's layers are one panel, `MapLayers` (`src/components/map-layers.tsx`, sources and rules in `src/lib/map-layers.ts`): kk7 thermals and skyways by time of year and of day, rain now (RainViewer), clouds now (the satellite that sees the map's middle: GOES and Himawari through NASA GIBS, Meteosat from EUMETSAT's map service, visible light by day and infrared at night) and lightning now (NOAA nowCOAST over the Americas and the Pacific, Meteosat's lightning camera over Europe and Africa). The browser asks those sources itself (all allow it) and shows each picture's age. Airspace from openAIP goes through `/api/airspace/{z}/{x}/{y}`, which adds the optional `OPENAIP_API_KEY` secret and keeps tiles a day. The root loader's `airspace` flag shows the layer only once the secret is set. Choices are kept in `localStorage`. No layer changes a verdict.
